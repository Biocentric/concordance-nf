# Biocentric/concordance-nf: Citations

## Pipeline tools

- [somalier](https://github.com/brentp/somalier)

  > Pedersen BS, Bhetariya PJ, Brown J, Kravitz SN, Marth G, Jensen RL, Bronner MP, Underhill HR, Quinlan AR. Somalier: rapid relatedness estimation for cancer and germline studies using efficient genome sketches. Genome Medicine. 2020 Jul 13;12(1):62. doi: 10.1186/s13073-020-00761-2. PubMed PMID: 32664994; PubMed Central PMCID: PMC7359478.

- CHARR, the contamination estimator somalier implements

  > Wang Q, Dhindsa RS, Carss K, et al. CHARR efficiently estimates contamination from DNA sequencing data. American Journal of Human Genetics. 2023 Dec 7;110(12):2068-2076. doi: 10.1016/j.ajhg.2023.10.011.

- The KING-robust kinship estimator that somalier's relatedness follows

  > Manichaikul A, Mychaleckyj JC, Rich SS, Daly K, Sale M, Chen WM. Robust relationship inference in genome-wide association studies. Bioinformatics. 2010 Nov 15;26(22):2867-73. doi: 10.1093/bioinformatics/btq559.

- [SAMtools / HTSlib](https://www.htslib.org/)

  > Danecek P, Bonfield JK, Liddle J, Marshall J, Ohan V, Pollard MO, Whitwham A, Keane T, McCarthy SA, Davies RM, Li H. Twelve years of SAMtools and BCFtools. Gigascience. 2021 Feb 16;10(2):giab008. doi: 10.1093/gigascience/giab008.

- [MultiQC](https://multiqc.info/)

  > Ewels P, Magnusson M, Lundin S, Käller M. MultiQC: summarize analysis results for multiple tools and samples in a single report. Bioinformatics. 2016 Oct 1;32(19):3047-8. doi: 10.1093/bioinformatics/btw354.
